WHAT IS IN THIS PACKAGE
-----------------------
Nickchat.exe   Windows app. Double-click it and Nickchat opens in its own window,
               not in a browser. It needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which is
               built into Windows 11 and up-to-date Windows 10.
Nickchat.html  The whole messenger as one file. Open it in any browser.
credits.txt    This file.


FEATURES
--------
- Sign up and log in with a nickname and a password. A nickname belongs to the first person who signs up
  with it, so nobody else can use it. Dark mode is the default.
- Profile photos, accent colours and wallpapers, big emoji with bursts, swipe to reply,
  typing dots, search across all chats, camera switch and a draggable video window in calls.
- Status updates for 24 hours with view counts, a Calls tab with missed calls, and a liquid glass tab bar.
- Voice calls and video calls between two people (mute, camera on/off, call history in the chat).
- Add contacts by nickname and chat one-to-one.
- Groups of up to 30 people.
- Sent, delivered and read ticks.
- Typing indicator, online status and last seen.
- Replies, emoji reactions, edit, delete for me and delete for everyone.
- Pin, mute, block, search, drafts, unread counts.
- Light and dark theme, notifications, sound.
- Chat backup and restore.


THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND FONTS
------------------------------
- ntfy.sh: free public message relay that carries messages and call set-up between people.
  https://ntfy.sh
- Google and Cloudflare STUN servers and the free Open Relay TURN service help calls connect.
  Audio and video go directly between the two devices and are encrypted.
- Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans: fonts loaded from Google Fonts,
  both under the SIL Open Font License.
  https://fonts.google.com


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Chats are stored only in the browser you use. Use Settings > Back up chats
  to keep a copy.
- Messages pass through a public relay, so do not send secrets.
- Your password is never sent anywhere. Only a scrambled code of it is stored on the relay to lock your
  nickname, and a forgotten password cannot be recovered. If you stay away for more than about 12 hours,
  the lock can expire and someone else could take the nickname. Open the app now and then.
- Calls can fail on very strict networks. Allow the microphone and camera when asked.
- A nickname is not locked to one person. Anyone can type the same nickname.
- Messages wait on the relay for about 12 hours if someone is offline.
- If the WebView2 Runtime is missing, Nickchat.exe offers to open its free
  download page.
- Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" notice the first time you run
  Nickchat.exe, because the file is not digitally signed. Choose
  "More info", then "Run anyway".
